⚠ ACTION REQUIRED BEFORE TRAVEL (11 days away!)
  • VEP (Vehicle Entry Permit) — ALL Singapore-registered cars must have a VEP to enter Malaysia. Processing takes 5–14 days. Apply now at vep.jpj.gov.my if not done. Failure = RM300 fine at border.
  • VEP-RFID must be linked to Touch 'n Go (TNG) eWallet for the RM20 road entry charge — top up TNG to at least RM200.
  • Pre-book Malaysia Heritage Studios via Ticket2U — can reach capacity on weekends.
  • Call Funny Haunted Adventure (Port Dickson) before visiting — the venue has had past closures; verify it is currently open.

Fill Petrol Before Crossing (Singapore law: min ¾ tank)
24-hour stations near Woodlands: Shell Woodlands Avenue 3, Caltex Marsiling, SPC along Woodlands Road. Fill to full — cheaper than Malaysia RON97 is still a good deal, but you must start with ¾ tank minimum.
Singapore law — enforced. Fine if below ¾ tank at checkpoint.

Singapore Immigration — Woodlands Checkpoint (WCP)
At 4am Saturday, expect ~10–20 minutes. Scan passports/IC at kiosk, proceed. Note: some commercial lorry traffic at this hour but passenger car lanes move faster.
~10–20 min clearance

Malaysia Immigration — Sultan Iskandar Building (CIQ), JB
At 4am Saturday, typically 15–30 minutes. Declare nothing dutiable. After clearing, navigate through JB city to reach PLUS highway northbound (Skudai / Jalan Tun Abdul Razak → PLUS interchange). At 4am, JB traffic is minimal.
~15–30 min clearance + ~15 min to PLUS highway Use Waze — it guides you through JB to PLUS entry point

Highway is almost empty at 5am. Drive at 100–105 km/h (speed cameras active). Ayer Keroh R&R is your last rest stop before the Ayer Keroh exit — use it if anyone needs the toilet. Kids will likely be asleep.
~2 hours to Ayer Keroh exit Ayer Keroh R&R is just before your exit — convenient toilet stop

Driving Guide for Singaporeans
Trip Route Overview
Singapore → ~3h → Melaka → ~1h 10min → Port Dickson → ~1h 10min → Melaka → ~2h → JB → ~30–90min → Singapore
Total driving ~10–11 hours across 9 days. Toll estimate SG↔Melaka (return): ~RM55–65. Use PLUS highway throughout.

VEP (Vehicle Entry Permit) — URGENT

  1. Mandatory from 1 Jul 2025 for all Singapore-registered cars entering Malaysia by land
  2. Apply at vep.jpj.gov.my — processing 5–14 business days
  3. Collect physical RFID tag and stick to headlamp as instructed
  4. Link RFID to your Touch 'n Go (TNG) eWallet — RM20 road charge auto-deducted on entry
  5. Failure = RM300 fine at border. Apply TODAY — trip is in 11 days.

Touch 'n Go eWallet Setup

  1. Download TNG eWallet (iOS/Android) and register
  2. Top up minimum RM200 for the trip (RM20 road entry + ~RM55 tolls return + parking)
  3. Link your VEP-RFID tag to the wallet
  4. TNG eWallet also handles most car park payments in Malaysia
  5. PLUS highway toll booths accept TNG card/eWallet — keep in reach

Petrol Rules

  1. Singapore law: must have at least ¾ tank when exiting Singapore (enforced since 1991)
  2. Fill up at a Singapore petrol station before crossing
  3. In Malaysia: Singaporeans must pump RON97 (unsubsidised) — NOT RON95
  4. RON97 is still significantly cheaper than Singapore petrol — fill up whenever convenient
  5. Petronas, Shell, BHPetrol widely available on PLUS highway

Border Crossing Tips

  1. Tuas Second Link is generally faster than Woodlands Causeway on weekends (Sat mornings, Sun evenings)
  2. Bring Singapore IC/passport + car insurance documents
  3. Check Singapore car insurance covers Malaysia (most comprehensive policies do)
  4. Sunday return: use Tuas 2nd Link. Woodlands peak: 4–9pm Sun — 1.5–2hr queues
  5. Leave JB before 12pm or after 9pm on Sunday to avoid worst queues

Driving in Malaysia

  1. Speed limits: 110 km/h highway | 90 km/h federal roads | 50 km/h town
  2. AES speed cameras common on PLUS — drive at 100–105 km/h to be safe
  3. Use Waze (best for live traffic + camera alerts in Malaysia)
  4. Download offline maps as backup
  5. Rest stops: Ayer Keroh R&R and Machap R&R (clean, toilets, food, petrol)

Parking Tips

  1. Jonker Street weekends: Park at Mahkota Parade or Dataran Pahlawan mall — 5–10 min walk; or use Grab
  2. The Shore Oceanarium: Free parking at The Shore mall
  3. Mydin MITC: Parking RM1
  4. Klebang attractions: Free street parking available near Submarine Museum and MoCity
  5. Most major attractions in Ayer Keroh have free or cheap parking lots
